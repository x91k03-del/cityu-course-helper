              2. Electives (27 or 36 credit units)


              Electives: minimum 27 credit units from these electives
              Students may choose any of the Artificial Intelligence / Data Science / Cybersecurity /
              Multimedia Computing / Software Engineering and Project Management streams by taking 3
              courses of the selected stream and any 6 elective courses from the list.
              Students admitted via 1070 (JS1070) are automatically placed under the AI, Computing and
              Transformation stream, and are required to take the DSC3026 and CS3505 as the stream
              cores.
              For those who do not want to focus on a selected stream, they can take any 9 elective courses
              from the list.
              Students who choose to enrol in CS3000 are required to take additional 3 elective courses from
              the list in lieu of the 9-credit CS3505.
