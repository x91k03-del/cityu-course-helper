Part II Course Details
Abstract
This course traces the origins of contemporary conceptions of romantic love in works of literature and popular culture
drawn from a wide range of historical periods and cultures, extending from ancient times to the current day. Beginning with
early notions of love in ancient Greek philosophy and literature, the course traces the evolution of love through medieval
traditions of courtly love and the beginnings of modern notions of love in Shakespeare's plays to love as conceived in 20th
and 21st-century ﬁlms, television dramas and pop songs. In studying how people all over the world have thought about love
in diﬀerent eras, students will gain a sense of the development of human thinking about emotions across time and space
and will learn to appreciate continuities and diﬀerences between various cultures and time periods. Through thinking and
writing critically about love, this course will provide students with a new perspective on this all-important emotion.
