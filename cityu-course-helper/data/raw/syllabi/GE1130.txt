2         GE1130: Introduction to Digital Media
Part II Course Details
Abstract
In the age of digital revolution, everyone is focusing on digital media and technology, such as internet, video and audio
streaming, web 2.0, 3G computer networks, media art , e-learning, mobile media, digital marketing, human-computer
interface (HCI) …etc.
As a new generation of learners, we should have a good understanding on digital media, principles of user interface design,
production planning, production skill, prototype design and development of interactive media.
This course covers practical learning in video production and UXUI prototype design/multimedia project with various types
of human computer interaction techniques (sensors, motion capture, augmented reality) .
