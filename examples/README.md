# Recorded test inputs and outputs

F, N and M contain fictional CM transcript PDFs/text, the raw Qwen response,
validated objective parameters, and the external optimiser's full result.
Each transcript has 15 credits in Year 1A and 15 credits in Year 1B.

`../reports/transcript-parameter-pipeline/paired_inputs.txt` records both exact
input formats. `after_comparison.json` reports the three matching parameter/path
pairs. These three examples were taught after failures and are corrective retests.
Eight separate synthetic record/weight exports and four new English messages are
saved under `training/` and `english-correction/`; their reviewed policy is familiar.

The optimiser, rather than the language model, selects the courses. All three
finite-context plans reach 122 credits, but 25 future credits still have pending
class details. These are curriculum simulations, not confirmed registrations.
