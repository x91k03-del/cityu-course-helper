# Third-party materials

The trained checkpoint is a LoRA adaptation of Qwen/Qwen3-8B at revision
`b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218`. The upstream model licence is
preserved in `third_party/Qwen3-8B-LICENSE.txt`. The frozen base weights are
restored from the official model repository by `tools/restore_base_model.py`.

Course syllabi, programme documents and timetable snapshots retain their original
City University of Hong Kong and other identified source owners' rights. Their
provenance is recorded in `sources/` and the parsed records. The private project
archive does not claim ownership of these materials or grant redistribution rights.

Student inputs in the examples and evaluation datasets are fictional. The actual
sample student transcript, authentication credentials and browser/session profiles
are excluded from this repository.
