Part II Course Details
Abstract
2         CS2611: Seminars on Contemporary Technology I
This course aims to increase the breadth of outlook of students in computer related topics. Upon completion, students
should be able to:
1.  know the current technological trends, social and ethical issues in computer related professions;
2.  acquire knowledge through self-motivated, continuous and outside-class-room learning.
